The reality of running a preorder business

Getting the orders is one thing. Managing everything afterwards is another.

A customer places an order. Another sends a deposit. Your supplier confirms some products, delays others. Your shipment is on its way, but customers are already asking when their orders will arrive.

Meanwhile, you're checking WhatsApp, updating spreadsheets and trying to remember who ordered what.

Sound familiar?

  • Orders scattered everywhere

    Customer orders, payments and product details live in different places.

  • No clear picture of your business

    It's difficult to know what's ordered, what's available and what's still outstanding.

  • Imports become complicated

    Keeping track of products, import batches and shipments becomes harder as your business grows.

  • Fulfilment becomes stressful

    You need to know which customer ordered what and which orders are ready to go.

Your business has grown beyond what spreadsheets and memory can comfortably handle.

It's time for a better way to work.
